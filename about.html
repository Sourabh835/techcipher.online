About TechCipher

TechCipher started with a simple frustration: most tech and cybersecurity content online is either one of two things. It is written like a textbook for people who already know the subject, or it is hype designed to sell a course.

We write for the middle ground. Beginners in India who want honest, practical answers. What is AI actually doing? How does cybercrime really work? How do you build a career in security without getting scammed by a training center?

Every article is researched, kept up to date, and written with the reader in mind. No AI slop, no fluff, no fake promises.
